Our Story

Apex BG Developments LLC is a Texas-based design management company supporting commercial and residential projects across multiple municipalities. Based in Texas, with design-management experience on projects in Oklahoma, Florida, California, New Mexico and other states. The company coordinates our designers and professionals and manages plans and permitting against jurisdiction-specific review standards. Apex BG is built on efficiency, accountability, and work that holds up under real-world permitting and regulatory scrutiny.

How We Work

We keep the process straightforward: understand the scope, identify jurisdictional requirements, and produce documents that satisfy clients and the city through permitting. We communicate directly and stay involved through permit approval, so projects keep moving.
